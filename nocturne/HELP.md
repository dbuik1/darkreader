# Nocturne help

Nocturne shows websites in dark colors. It is on for every site as soon as you install it.

## Turn it on or off

- **Everywhere:** open the Nocturne button in the toolbar and switch Nocturne off, or press Alt+Shift+D.
- **For one site:** open the Nocturne button on that site and switch off "Darken" followed by the site name, or press Alt+Shift+A.
- **Choose dark or light:** use Mode in the Nocturne button. Light mode gives sites light colors instead.

To pin the button, select the puzzle piece in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin next to Nocturne.

## A page looks wrong

1. Open the Nocturne button on that page.
2. Under Adjust colors, try a lower contrast or brightness.
3. If the page still looks wrong, switch off "Darken" followed by the site name.

## Pages Nocturne can't change

Chrome doesn't let extensions change its own pages, such as Settings, the new tab page and the Chrome Web Store. The Nocturne button says so when you open it on one of them.

## Change shortcuts

Open Settings, Shortcuts, then select Change shortcuts. Chrome opens its own shortcut page in a new tab.

## Privacy

Nocturne processes pages on your device and has no analytics or ads. Read the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).
