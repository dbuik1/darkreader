# Nocturne privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Nocturne is a browser extension that shows websites in dark colors. It does not collect, sell or share your personal data, and it has no analytics or ads.

## What happens on your device

- Nocturne reads the pages you open so it can change their colors, images, canvases and PDFs. This happens on your device. Page content is not sent to the developer or to anyone else.
- Your settings, such as the color mode and the sites you turned off, are stored by your browser's extension storage.

## When Nocturne uses the network

- **Settings sync.** "Sync settings across devices" is on by default. Your settings are then saved to the account you are signed in to in your browser, through the browser's own sync. Turn it off under Settings, Backup and reset.
- **Images and stylesheets.** To work out a page's colors, Nocturne sometimes downloads an image or stylesheet that the page itself uses, from the same site the page loads it from.
- **PDF files.** If you turn on the dark PDF viewer, Nocturne downloads the PDF you opened from its address so it can display it.
- **Site fixes.** "Download site fixes" is off by default. When you turn it on, Nocturne downloads lists of per-site adjustments from Dark Reader's public GitHub repository. No information about you or your browsing is sent with that request beyond what any web request contains.

Nocturne does not run code downloaded from the internet.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why Nocturne needs it |
| --- | --- |
| Access to all websites | To change the colors of any page you visit |
| Scripting | To darken tabs that were already open when you installed or turned on Nocturne |
| Storage | To save your settings |
| Alarms | To switch dark mode on and off on a schedule you set |
| Font settings | To list your installed fonts when you choose a font override |
| Context menus (optional) | To add Nocturne to the right-click menu, only if you turn that on |
